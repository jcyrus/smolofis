# Administration console surface

Mode: Operate. Audience: the small-office administrator described in PRODUCT.md.
The user approved direct implementation and screenshot review, with the console
behind a disabled flag and outstanding platform checks retained as release gates.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet office home that reveals the next useful administrative task.
Use an app launcher and full-page tools, rather than a simulated window manager.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory, white surfaces, deep forest text, and muted green actions.
System sans typography, consistent outlined icons, softly rounded launch tiles,
and a restrained sage-to-sand wallpaper band on the overview.

STORY: Understand monitored service health, open a tool, inspect diagnostics, and
see clearly when account or folder administration has not been connected.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact left navigation, a generous page title, a state-aware
wallpaper band, four task destinations, and service launchers. On a phone, retain
the hierarchy and move primary navigation to a five-item bottom bar. The signature
interaction is expanding a service row into useful diagnostics and copying them.

FORM: Task launcher with dedicated administration pages; derived from the user's
confirmed umbrelOS-inspired home and mobile full-page tools. No random direction
selection: composition and interaction were explicitly settled in conversation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
